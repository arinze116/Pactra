# Pactra 60-Second Pitch

Small businesses often manage payments through forms, chat messages, and
spreadsheets. That makes it difficult to prove who was allowed to spend, why a
payment was approved, or whether the financial record is complete.

Pactra gives the business one governed payment workspace. It defines spending
limits and restricted vendors up front, reserves funds deterministically,
routes exceptions for human approval, and records the final receipt, policy
trace, ledger entries, and audit lifecycle.

AI can turn a natural-language request into a typed application action, but it
does not have financial authority. Pactra validates every argument and runs
the same policy, ownership, approval, idempotency, and ledger rules used by the
manual form. If Gemini is unavailable, deterministic fallback keeps governance
working.

Today Pactra is sandbox-only: simulated internal funds and an optional PayPal
Sandbox rail. The product demonstrates a safer control layer for businesses
that want faster payment operations without giving automation an unchecked
ability to spend.
