# Pactra interface direction

## Product

Pactra is a payment-control workspace for operations teams issuing and reviewing PayPal actions under deterministic policy.

## Character

Controlled, exact, quietly assured.

## World

The interface belongs to an operations ledger and risk-control desk: calm neutral surfaces, compact controls, precise figures, and clear decision states.

## Moments

- Today's payment exposure, shown as a concise set of operational figures and category utilisation.
- The live decision ledger, where approved, blocked, and review-required payments are immediately distinguishable.

## Signature

A restrained green decision indicator marks active policy and approved outcomes. Semantic amber and red appear only when review or intervention is required.

## Design system

- Typography: IBM Plex Sans Variable for the interface; tabular figures use the configured mono stack.
- Canvas: warm graphite-tinted off-white with white working surfaces and a dark ink sidebar.
- Accent: deep operational green used sparingly for active policy, focus, and successful decisions.
- Spacing: 4px base grid; 16px compact groups, 24px card padding, 32px page rhythm.
- Radius: 8px controls, 12px cards, 16px major panels.
- Elevation: hairline borders and small neutral shadows only.
- Motion: one short page entrance; purposeful loading rotation; reduced-motion support.

## Screen inventory

1. Payment operations overview
2. New payment command panel
3. Decision ledger with filters and approval review
4. Active policy and category capacity summary

## Responsive behaviour

- Desktop: persistent 240px navigation, ledger-led content grid, command panel on the right.
- Tablet: compact top navigation and stacked working panels.
- Mobile: two-column figures, horizontally scrollable decision filters, and card-based ledger rows.
